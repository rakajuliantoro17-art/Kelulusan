# QR Service

QR Service mengelola seluruh proses QR Code pada SARPRASIN.

## Fitur

- Generate QR Asset
- Parse QR
- Validasi QR
- Verifikasi Keaslian
- Scan Result Processing
- Cache QR
- Integrasi Asset
- Public Asset Check

## Flow

Asset

↓

QR Generator

↓

QR Code

↓

Scan

↓

QR Parser

↓

Asset Service

↓

Result
